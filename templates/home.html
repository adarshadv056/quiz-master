What the app does

Everything needed for a complete quiz workflow.

Quiz Master keeps the college-project scope simple, but presents it like a polished academic product.

01

Subject and chapter library

Admins can organize quiz content by subject and chapter so students always know what topic they are attempting.

02

Quiz scheduling

Quizzes open based on their quiz date, keeping the student dashboard focused on what is currently available.

03

Question management

Create multiple-choice questions, edit quiz details, and maintain the question set from the admin workspace.

04

Score history

Students can review previous attempts and see how their quiz performance changes over time.

How it works

A clear path from setup to result.

Build

Admin creates subjects, chapters, quizzes, and questions.

Attempt

Student opens an available quiz, previews details, and starts the attempt.

Review

The app stores scores and shows results, summaries, and charts.

Designed for two roles

One app, two focused dashboards.

Admins get control over quiz content. Students get a clean view of what to attempt and how they performed.

Admin dashboard

  • Manage subjects and chapters
  • Create and edit quizzes
  • Add questions and answer options
  • View users and summary charts

Student dashboard

  • See available quizzes
  • Preview quiz details before starting
  • Attempt timed quizzes
  • Review scores and progress
